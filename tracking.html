03 / Make sense of it

Between the scans.

A few common updates, explained.

Label created / information received

The shipping label exists, but the carrier may not have scanned the parcel yet. Check your agent's shipment details to confirm handover.

In transit

Your parcel is moving through the delivery network. There may be gaps between scans, especially during international transport.

Customs processing

The parcel is being processed at the border. Check the carrier's official tracking page for any request for information or payment.

Out for delivery

The parcel has reached the local delivery stage. Check the carrier's latest update for instructions or delivery changes.

Delivered, but not received

Check the delivery photo, safe places and other people at the address. Contact the delivery carrier if you still cannot find it.

No results or no new scans

Confirm the number with your agent and check for a separate international or local tracking number. If the update remains unclear, contact your agent or carrier; this helper cannot determine whether a parcel is lost.
