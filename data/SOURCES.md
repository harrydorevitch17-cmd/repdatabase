# Geographic data

Snapshot downloaded 24 October 2026.

- cities.json: GeoNames cities15000, https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities15000.zip . CC BY (see GeoNames terms); https://www.geonames.org/export/ . Names, alternate names, country, administrative code, population and city-centre coordinates only. Approximate city matching never identifies a parcel GPS position.
- airports.json: OurAirports airports.csv, https://ourairports.com/data/ . Public domain. Medium/large airports with scheduled service and an IATA code. Airport proximity does not establish a parcel booking or shipping route.
- Leaflet 1.9.4 in ../vendor: BSD-2-Clause; licence included. Tiles, when requested, are served by OpenStreetMap under its tile usage policy and displayed with attribution. No bulk tile downloads.

- world.json: Natural Earth 1:110m country polygons, public domain; https://www.naturalearthdata.com/ . Properties trimmed to country name. Used as the offline basemap.
