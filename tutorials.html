There are two payments.

01 / BUYING

Find it. Choose it. Order it.

  1. Open a find in Kakobuy

    Browse the product index and select Buy on Kakobuy. Sign in or create an account on Kakobuy. If a product does not load, copy its original listing link and try it in Kakobuy's search.

  2. Check the exact option

    Choose your colour, size and quantity. Compare the seller's measurements with a piece you already own; do not rely on the size letter alone. Confirm the selected option and current price before paying.

  3. Wait for warehouse arrival

    Kakobuy purchases from the seller, who sends the item to the warehouse in China. Follow the order in your Kakobuy account. Seller dispatch and warehouse arrival are separate from international shipping.

02 / QUALITY CHECK

Look before you ship.

QC means quality check: inspection photos of the item that reached the warehouse. Review your own order's photos before submitting a parcel.

  • Right item?Compare the colour, style and size label with your order.
  • Right fit?Check any measured photos against the seller's chart. Request a measurement if the fit is unclear.
  • Anything visible?Look for marks, holes, uneven stitching and missing parts. Zoom in on details that matter to you.
  • Need a closer look?Ask Kakobuy about additional photos or a return before shipping. Check the current fees and seller's return eligibility in your account.

Photos can help you judge appearance. They cannot guarantee authenticity, fit, durability or hidden condition. Other buyers' QC photos are references; your warehouse photos show your order.

03 / SHIPPING

Build the parcel. Bring it home.

  1. Select your warehouse items

    Once you are happy with the items, select them in your Kakobuy warehouse and submit a parcel. Review your delivery address and packing options carefully.

  2. Compare the available shipping lines

    Use the options shown for your destination and parcel. Compare price, estimated delivery window, tracking and any coverage offered. Weight and parcel dimensions can affect the final shipping charge.

  3. Pay and follow the parcel

    Review the final charges in Kakobuy and pay for shipping. Once dispatched, use the tracking number and carrier shown in your parcel details. Our own tracking tool is currently under maintenance.

04 / QUICK ANSWERS

The bits people get stuck on.

Do I buy from repdatabase?

repdatabase helps you find products. The Buy on Kakobuy button takes you to Kakobuy to place and manage the order. Product sellers supply the items.

Why does the price look different on Kakobuy?

Our converted prices are estimates. Check the current listing price, selected option, exchange rate and fees in Kakobuy before paying.

Does buying the item include delivery to my house?

The item purchase and international parcel shipping are separate. You arrange and pay for international shipping after the item reaches the warehouse.

What if the item is wrong or the seller has not sent it?

Open the order in Kakobuy and contact its support team. Ask about the order status or available return options before submitting the item for international shipping.

Where do I find the latest service rules?

Use Kakobuy's current help centre and your account for inspection rules, storage periods, returns and shipping options. See the official inspection and warehouse rules.
